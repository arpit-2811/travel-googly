✦ ABOUT TRAVEL GOOGLY

Crafting Authentic Journeys & Unforgettable Holidays

Founded by Pankaj Sharma, Travel Googly is a customer-first Indian travel agency dedicated to hassle-free holiday planning, transparent pricing in ₹ INR, and 24×7 human support on WhatsApp.

OUR JOURNEY

A Vision for Human-Centered Travel

In 2018, Travel Googly was founded with a straightforward mission: Indian travelers deserve personalized holiday planning without confusing hidden fees, rigid pre-set schedules, or automated bot responses.

From our head office in Morena, Madhya Pradesh, we have successfully organized holidays and sacred pilgrimages for over 2,000+ happy travelers across India. Whether you are arranging a senior citizen Char Dham Yatra, a romantic beach villa stay in Bali, or an adventurous mountain road trip in Ladakh, we treat every itinerary with personal attention.

"Travel should be joyful, memorable, and transparent. We work as your personal trip advisors — finding the best hotels, reliable cabs, and optimal routes so you simply enjoy the journey."
— Pankaj Sharma, Founder & Operations Lead
